## Why

The advanced-team study found useful rules that the planner cannot select or finish, so adding more skill exposure alone cannot improve its play. The first tactical milestone should close three bounded action gaps and protect Vampire possession before expanding to throwing and weapon plans.

## What Changes

- Make Secure the Ball, Hypnotic Gaze and Multiple Block available through the headless candidate seam and planner continuations.
- Compose small capability modules with the existing safe-first planner; judge current players, targets, possession, support and remaining activations.
- Account for the fixed Secure the Ball test, Multiple Block strength reduction and Hypnotic Gaze's loss of tackle zones.
- Keep failed-Bloodlust Vampires near legal bite support where possible, and price unsupported risky activations.
- Verify beneficial, harmful and neutral boards, then compare the candidate with a frozen pre-change coach over fresh mirrored Daily Driver matches. Retain race-level and bash-specific objectives and publish a readable HTML comparison.
