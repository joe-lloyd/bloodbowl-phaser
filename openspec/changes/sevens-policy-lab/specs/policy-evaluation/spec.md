## Purpose

Run reproducible tactical and full-match experiments which distinguish legal capabilities, useful AI choices and performance evidence.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Repeatable staged evaluation

The lab SHALL run public-entry tactical fixtures before smoke and held-out full-match confirmation. Each stage SHALL retain machine-readable status, evidence and failure reasons, and an incompatible source change SHALL invalidate resumed acquisition.

#### Scenario: Failed tactical gate

- **WHEN** the coach loses its receiver target or selects a rejected command
- **THEN** the gate fails with a reproducible fixture and no promotion verdict is issued

### Requirement: Honest skill evidence contracts

Every skill and trait SHALL have a named contract recording AI-choice evidence, passive or mechanical evidence, lifecycle scope and remaining gaps as applicable. Forced scripts SHALL NOT count as unforced AI choice.

#### Scenario: Missing tactical evidence

- **WHEN** a skill is present but the policy has no verified useful selection
- **THEN** the report retains an open tactical gap instead of certifying useful coverage

### Requirement: Fair frozen comparisons

Confirmation SHALL use exact candidate and opponent source digests, disjoint seeds, complete policy/side/receiving blocks and replay-verified outcomes. Reports SHALL distinguish equal-roster estimates from template-weighted totals and retain sparse cells and excluded blocks.

#### Scenario: Candidate changes after acquisition

- **WHEN** a review correction changes the policy
- **THEN** the prior comparison remains attached to its original source and does not establish performance of the corrected head

### Requirement: Opportunity and outcome reporting

The lab SHALL distinguish legal availability, checked useful opportunities, proposed plans, selections, accepted continuations and observed tactical endpoints. Reports SHALL include scoring, defensive and player-loss outcomes with the supported context.

#### Scenario: No passing

- **WHEN** full matches contain no selected passes
- **THEN** the report shows whether opportunities or proposals were missing and preserves examples needed to diagnose the gap

### Requirement: Reachable visual progress

The implementation SHALL publish a dark visual stage update and final evidence through the existing HTMLify route, including source provenance and links to detailed data.

#### Scenario: Automated stage completion

- **WHEN** a scripted stage finishes
- **THEN** its saved status and report can be rebuilt without repeating games or model analysis
