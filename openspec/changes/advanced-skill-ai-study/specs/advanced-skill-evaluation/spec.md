## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Legal reproducible advanced teams

The study SHALL use existing public templates and progression legality to build deterministic focused and randomized variants. Matched randomized allocations MUST preserve the focused allocation's player, category, access and value band.

#### Scenario: Rebuilding an advanced variant

- **WHEN** a manifest variant is reconstructed
- **THEN** its added skills, advancement records, team value and native rerolls match the frozen inventory
- **AND** no duplicate, incompatible or inaccessible skill is added

### Requirement: Balanced and auditable matches

The study SHALL save every scheduled game and an actual initial-state command trace. Completed results MUST reconcile authoritative events and pass replay verification. Comparisons SHALL identify their cost and matching assumptions.

#### Scenario: A mirrored comparison completes

- **WHEN** both legs for a seed finish
- **THEN** the same variants appear on opposite physical sides
- **AND** the summary uses complete pairs for uncertainty estimates

### Requirement: Contextual skill evidence

The study SHALL distinguish natural effects, roster exposure, controlled mechanical probes and actual AI selection. It MUST retain rejected or unavailable ablation scripts without treating them as failed skill outcomes.

#### Scenario: An enabling skill is removed

- **WHEN** the corresponding command is no longer legal
- **THEN** the result is recorded as unavailable capability
- **AND** it is excluded from comparable skill-present/absent endpoint estimates

### Requirement: Readable complete coverage plan

The offline HTML dashboard SHALL summarize the findings using charts and plain-language explanations and provide searchable coverage for every catalogue skill and trait. Counts and rates MUST retain their units, with full raw data downloadable.

#### Scenario: Inspecting a missing natural effect

- **WHEN** a skill has no natural trigger event
- **THEN** its row distinguishes absent players, passive telemetry, action planning and non-match scope where supported by evidence
- **AND** it supplies a tactical hypothesis and a next validation task rather than claiming the rule is missing
