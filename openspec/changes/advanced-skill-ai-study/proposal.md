## Why

The first 7,776-game study is difficult to digest and omits advancement-only skills from natural team play. We need readable findings and controlled advanced-team evidence before expanding the scripted AI's tactical modules.

## What Changes

- Present the baseline and advanced study in a concise offline HTML dashboard with charts, plain-language findings, searchable coverage and downloadable data.
- Create legal advanced variants of all public templates, with role-aware skill packages and deterministic random-skill controls.
- Run mirrored full matches and skill-present/absent scenario probes on Daily Driver, retaining manifests, actual initial states, event counts and replay verification.
- Produce an explicit plan for every skill and trait, distinguishing engine support, roster exposure, natural AI use, passive telemetry gaps and tactical planning gaps.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `advanced-skill-evaluation`: Reproducible advanced-team experiments, contextual probes, coverage roadmap and readable study dashboard.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The production AI and competition team library remain unchanged.

## Impact

Headless study scripts, a reusable rule-runner preparation hook, focused tests and documentation. Simulation compute uses Daily Driver; generated result artifacts remain outside source control. No new runtime dependencies or paid model calls.
