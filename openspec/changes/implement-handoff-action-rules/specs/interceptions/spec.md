## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Interception applies to thrown balls only

An interception opportunity SHALL arise only from a Pass Action's throw (including a Throw Bomb and a Quick Pass). A Hand-off SHALL NOT be interceptable: no eligibility test SHALL be run, no interceptor decision SHALL be offered, and no interception preview SHALL be drawn while a Hand-off is being targeted.

#### Scenario: Hand-off past an eligible opponent
- **WHEN** a player performs a Hand-off to an adjacent team-mate while a Standing opposition player with its Tackle Zone stands beside both of them
- **THEN** no interception is offered and the ball is transferred for the receiving Catch

#### Scenario: No interception preview during a hand-off
- **WHEN** a coach hovers over a legal Hand-off target
- **THEN** no interception corridor or threatened-square highlight is shown
