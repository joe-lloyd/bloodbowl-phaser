Every team, every player

Neon style, isometric, idle in the ready stance. Every player stands on one pitch square, and the square is across. That's the treeman's widest idle pose, so the treeman just fits and everyone else is to scale against it.

How to read this

  • Game pixels are the real in-game sprites: rendered at 25 pixels per metre and shown at 2×. Detail renders the same model at 50 pixels per metre, so you can judge the shapes.
  • Kit colours are placeholders, one neon pair per team. In the game each coach picks their own and the sprites are tinted at runtime.
  • Every model is built from simple shapes on a shared skeleton and generated from a short spec in scripts/sprites/team_models.py. Changing a helmet or a body plan and re-rendering one model takes about 20 seconds.
  • Players repeated across teams, such as the Ogre, the treeman and the Skaven, share one model.
