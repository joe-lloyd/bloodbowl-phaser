Dither makes the mudThe shader breaks every edge between two tones into a checker. At 42 px that checker covers much of the cloth, so red reads as speckle.
Tint crushes the kitThe game multiplies grey kit pixels by the team colour. Crimson's dark shade lands near the outline colour. The ramp tint in the top bar fixes that with no new art.
