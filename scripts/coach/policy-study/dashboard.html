What to test next

1. Tactical module gates

Secure the Ball, Gaze, Multiple Block and feeding support now have explicit plans. Require useful, harmful and neutral boards, legal continuations and repeatable outcomes for every additional module.

2. Throwing and landing

Test Right Stuff, Lethal Flight, Strong Arm and Bullseye together in legal throwing teams. Compare scoring throws with damaging throws, landing failure, lost possession and the thrower’s alternative action.

3. Weapons and disruption

Separate foul, Chainsaw, Stab, Vomit, Fire and Bomb plans. Price armour, injury, ejection, bribes, allies in danger and better ordinary blocks.

4. Generalisation

Then add punt, jump and reaction modules; Vampire opponent counters; new Hard/Legend opponents and exploiters. Keep tuning seeds separate from fresh confirmation seeds.
