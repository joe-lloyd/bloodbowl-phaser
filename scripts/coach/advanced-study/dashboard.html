Advanced builds, with the reason for the control

Each focused build adds six legal advancements, with at most two per player. The randomized control uses the same players, category access and elite-cost band. These are coverage-oriented packages, not claims about the strongest possible team.

The randomized allocation is an experiment control using chosen-advancement prices. It is separate from the application’s cheaper two-roll random-primary development system. No free inducements compensate the original teams.

What was tested, and what the numbers can tell us

Controlled scenarios run with the focal skill present and removed, using 32 fixed seeds each. Their actions are scripted. “Changed endpoint” means positions, possession, player status, injuries, rerolls or score differed; it is not a win-rate improvement.

Some removed skills make an action illegal. Those are capability losses and are separated from comparable outcomes. Rejected scripts and errors are retained. Matching seeds reduce variance but do not preserve identical dice sequences after different actions or reactions.

Shows up to 20 matching contexts, ordered by changed endpoints. The CSV retains every context. These fixtures include isolated and scenario-granted skills; their provenance is in the acquisition manifest.
Validity, format and scope

All natural matches start fresh in standard weather, use native rerolls and the default GW Sevens profile, and have no free simulation inducements or persistent team growth. Draft and post-match rules are tracked separately. One original Snotling recipe remains excluded by the default Insignificant draft limit.

For Brawlin’ Brutes, the historical objective remains 2 × touchdowns + 3 × SPP-eligible casualties. Other rosters retain their existing touchdown-based target. Targets against the old heuristic are not pass/fail thresholds against equal planners.

Per-skill tactical advice is a hypothesis supported by the rule fixtures. Whole-team results evaluate packages; they do not isolate the value of each skill. Passive effects need additional action/roll or opportunity telemetry before “unused” can be concluded.

A plan to cover every ability without one giant script

Load small capability modules from the players actually present on both teams. Keep legality in the engine, let modules propose useful actions, then score the combined plans. Team names can supply defaults; current skills, possession, support and threats decide applicability.

1 · Make the missing actions available

Extend candidate enumeration and planning intents together for throws, gaze, punts, secure-ball and Multiple Block. Add useful foul, leap and special-attack plans. Give kickoff choices their own policy.

Done when: each action has a beneficial board, a harmful board and a neutral control, and the AI chooses a useful target rather than merely triggering the rule.

2 · Compose skill and opponent context

Build focused modules for Vampire feeding, enemy support denial, activation reliability, mutation movement and ball handling, throwing/landing, marking, weapon/foul decisions and skill-specific reactions.

Done when: combinations work together, support moves before the risky activation where useful, and rules are valued by scoring, possession and injury consequences.

3 · Confirm improvements against a diverse frozen pool

Compare candidate algorithms against the current and previous generations, full-budget Hard/Legend and targeted exploiters. Use fresh confirmation seeds and hold out team/skill combinations.

Promotion checks: complete mirrored pairs, clean engine checks, race-macro performance, weak-roster protection, the existing bash objectives, fewer unfed-carrier losses and useful coverage. Avoid rewarding raw skill counts.

How to use the skill roadmap

Each of the 108 entries lists the relevant module, a useful situation, a counterexample, natural effects, advancement exposure and controlled evidence. Turn those rows into small planning puzzles and measure the downstream result before changing production weights.

No production policy has been promoted in this study. A new ability can be useful in a particular position without being a good development choice for every player.
