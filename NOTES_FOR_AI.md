- FIXED 2026-07-27 (fix-drive-transition-lifecycle): scoring a touchdown logged "[Orchestrator] No handler for phase: TOUCHDOWN", KO recovery silently produced a player who existed twice at once (one copy on the pitch, one stuck in the KO box), and setup then refused to complete because the team could not field seven. - FIXED 2026-07-27 (fix-drive-transition-lifecycle): starting a second match in the same browser session crashed at the kickoff with "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'queueDepthSort')" from `new BallSprite`. Cause was listener lifetime, not the sprite: the first match's KickoffPhaseHandler stayed subscribed to the shared EventBus and captured a destroyed scene. GameScene.shutdown now destroys the orchestrator (which exits the handler and unsubscribes it), every scene EventBus subscription is tracked and removed, ServiceContainer.reset() runs before a new local scene is built, and the sprite factories no-op with a warning on an inactive scene. Covered by __tests__/unit/controllers/sceneLifecycle.test.ts.

- FIXED 2026-07-27 (normalize-cloud-data-model): saved teams and competitions duplicated the same data three times over — a stored player carried its full statline, resolved skill objects with rulebook description text, keywords, category access, cost and team value alongside its actual progression, and a league/tournament entrant embedded a whole roster snapshot that never saw a player's later advancement. Players now persist only identity, progression (SPP/level/advancements/characteristic advances), injuries and career stats; `hydratePlayer`/`dehydratePlayer` (src/data/persistence/teamPersistence.ts) rebuild everything else from `RosterTemplates` on load, recomputing team value rather than storing it. A position no longer in its roster degrades to a marked placeholder instead of dropping the player. Both the localStorage and cloud team repositories read tolerantly (old full-object documents convert on read) and always write the normalized shape. Competition entrants now reference a team by id/owner instead of embedding it (src/competition/teamRefs.ts fetches the live roster at fixture launch), so advancement carries across fixtures; a team records `activeCompetitionId` and can only be in one active competition at a time. Player career stats (games played, touchdowns, completions, casualties, kills, MVPs, plus stored-only passes-attempted/squares-moved) fold from the match summary at post-match confirmation and show in Team Management.
