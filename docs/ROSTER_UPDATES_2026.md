# Roster update, 2 November 2026

The rules review on 25 October checked the 2025 rulebook. The High Elf
roster still used its Teams of Legend positionals. This update checks the
published roster releases and the roster corrections in the May 2026 FAQ.

## High Elves

The March 2026 roster replaces the old Thrower, Catcher and Blitzer with
Phoenix Warriors, White Lions and Dragon Princes. It adds no new skill
definitions. All their starting skills belong to the 2025 core rules.

- Phoenix Warriors cost 90,000 and keep Cloud Burster, Pass and Safe Pass.
- White Lions cost 110,000, have MA 7 and AV 9+, and start with Claws and
  Wrestle. The roster allows two. Their secondary categories are Passing
  and Strength.
- Dragon Princes cost 110,000, have MA 8, and start with Block, My Ball and
  Steady Footing. Their keywords include Blitzer and Runner. Their secondary
  category is Strength.
- Linemen retain their profile and price, with Strength as their only
  secondary category.

The roster remains in Sevens tier 1. Sevens prices and the four-positional
limit still apply. The 50,000 re-roll price printed on the full-size roster
does not replace the game's Sevens re-roll price.

Saved team players resolve to the replacement profiles on load, preserving
identity, SPP, advances and injuries. Their stored position names become the
new names on the next save. Existing players are never removed automatically;
an old squad with more than two Catchers becomes a squad with too many White
Lions and must resolve the positional limit before it is legal. Saved match
snapshots retain their original player profiles.

The new positions reuse the existing High Elf sprite models. Old sprite
names remain mapped for historical saved matches.

Sources: [official English designer overview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/ahaquwqp/what-exactly-do-the-high-elves-do-in-blood-bowl-their-designers-tell-all/)
and [official German roster, 27 March 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/ger_27-03_bloodbowl_high_elf_team_rules-8k2zeqpfqs-7mqr2xccae.pdf).
The roster PDF resolves skill-access differences between community tables.

## May roster corrections

The [official May 2026 errata](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_20-05_blood_bowl_faq_errata-gytvlserev-ngihd3chox.pdf),
page 1, lists these changes:

- Corrected here: Renegade Ogre Loner 3+, Dwarf Runner secondary Agility,
  and PA 4+ for the Orc roster's Goblin Lineman.
- Already present: Mighty Blow for the Renegade Ogre; removal of Mutation
  access from Human, Imperial Nobility and Old World Alliance Ogres; three
  Old World Alliance Halfling Hopefuls; Loner 4+ for its Treeman.

The same FAQ, page 4, confirms Steady Footing saves block knockdowns and
falls, allows team re-rolls, and does not save players Placed Prone by
Wrestle. The engine previously handled only some movement falls. This change
adds block saves, the Blitz block's Rush, Jump falls and the re-roll offer.

This is a roster-release check with the associated High Elf skill fixes,
rather than a complete re-audit of every ruling in the FAQ.

## Other releases checked

The release immediately before High Elves was the paired launch of
Bretonnians and Tomb Kings in November 2025. They appear together in the
[official launch announcement](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/glgpuoah/saturday-pre-orders-play-ball-in-a-new-season-of-blood-bowl/),
with Spike! Journals 19 and 20 respectively.

Both had Third Season profiles, but checking the local 2025 rulebook's
roster pages 162 and 185 found these remaining errors, now corrected:

- Bretonnian Knight Throwers start with Dauntless, Nerves of Steel and
  Pass. Removed the extra starting Safe Pass skill. They may still learn
  it through their primary Passing access.
- Tomb Kings Throwers have secondary Agility, Devious and Strength access.
  Removed Mutation access.
- All four Tomb Kings positions include the Human keyword. Added it.
- Tomb Guardians have Big Guy, Blocker, Human and Undead keywords.
  Replaced the incorrect Skeleton keyword and added Blocker.

The statlines, costs, starting skills on the other positions, skill access,
positional limits, leagues and Apothecary permissions otherwise match those
rulebook rosters. Tomb Kings retain their Masters of Undeath special rule.
The historical Anointed Thrower and Anointed Blitzer display names remain
stable for saved teams. The new Steady Footing fixes also apply to Grail
Knights. The August Sevens tier list is already present for all 30 teams.

Night Goblins were [announced on 18 September](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/3zbmjzgp/the-big-autumn-preview-night-goblins-hit-the-gridiron/).
As checked on 2 November, the official material still describes their full
rules as forthcoming in Spike! Journal 23. It does not publish a complete
roster or the Squig Rage rule. They remain pending until those rules are
available. No speculative roster or skill has been added.
